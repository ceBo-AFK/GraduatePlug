About GraduatePlug

GraduatePlug is an opportunity platform focused on helping South African graduates, students and young job seekers discover entry-level opportunities in one convenient place.

We bring together jobs, internships, learnerships, graduate programmes, WIL opportunities, vacation work and bursaries.

Our Focus

  • Entry-level jobs and graduate opportunities
  • Internships and learnerships
  • Graduate programmes and WIL placement
  • Bursaries and career resources
